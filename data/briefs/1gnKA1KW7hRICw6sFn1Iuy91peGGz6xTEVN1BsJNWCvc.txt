2. SERP Gap
Current advice treats global coverage as a simple 24/7 staffing problem. It misses risk-weighted scheduling, quality handovers, local-language needs, cross-platform context switching, and the difference between always-on presence and always-on incident readiness.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Fund risk coverage and clean handovers—not empty online hours. The article uses a follow-the-sun operating map that focuses human time on peak activity, security-sensitive windows, product launches, and escalation ownership.
