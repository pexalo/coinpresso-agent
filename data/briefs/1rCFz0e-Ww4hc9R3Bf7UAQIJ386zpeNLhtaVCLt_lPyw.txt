FAQ Section

Q1: Why does ChatGPT recommend Coinbase but not my crypto exchange? ChatGPT cites Coinbase because it has strong entity recognition (public company, SEC filings, consistent brand definition across thousands of sources), objective data pages (fee structures, supported assets), and extensive third-party validation from mainstream financial media. Your exchange likely lacks one or more of these trust signals.

Q2: Does paying for SEO help with AI search visibility? Traditional SEO and AI visibility are increasingly divergent. A page can rank #1 on Google but never be cited by ChatGPT. However, some SEO fundamentals (structured data, clear content hierarchy, topical authority) do benefit both. The key difference is that AI engines prioritize extractable, objective content over keyword-optimized marketing copy.

Q3: Can I "prompt engineer" my way into AI citations? No. AI citations are determined by the model's training data and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems, not by user prompts. You cannot manipulate which brands appear in AI answers. Instead, you must make your content the most authoritative, well-structured, and trustworthy source for your category.

Q4: How do I know if ChatGPT is citing my crypto brand? Use AI visibility monitoring tools like Profound, Goodie, or Daydream to track how your brand appears across AI platforms. Alternatively, manually test 20-30 category-relevant prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini to establish a baseline citation share.

Q5: Is there a way to report incorrect AI citations about my crypto project? Most AI platforms have feedback mechanisms, but they are not reliable for brand management. The better strategy is to create such authoritative, well-structured content that AI engines naturally prefer your version of the truth over incorrect sources.
