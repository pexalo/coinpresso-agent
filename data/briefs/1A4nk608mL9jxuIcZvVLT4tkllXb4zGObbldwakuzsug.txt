Crypto Presale Referrals and Bonuses: Red Flags to Avoid

Meta description
Design presale referral and bonus structures with transparent terms, verifiable eligibility, disclosed incentives, and aligned smart-contract logic—without manipulative growth mechanics.

2. SERP Gap
Most campaign guides treat bonuses as a conversion lever. They ignore the trust cost of undisclosed rewards, opaque caps, mismatched contract terms, pressure tactics, and structures that reward recruitment more than product understanding.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Turn incentive design into a claim-and-code audit. The marketing terms, referral logic, eligibility rules, vesting, caps, and user-facing disclosures should all tell the same story.
