Meta description
Avoid the post-TGE marketing mistakes that turn presale attention into confusion: no utility narrative, weak support, unclear unlocks, unprepared community, and no product follow-through.

2. SERP Gap
Most presale content ends at the raise or listing. It does not address the transition from scarcity-led attention to product education, support, utility communication, unlock clarity, and durable community operations.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
The raise-listing gap is a narrative handover failure. The article gives teams a post-TGE operating plan that replaces acquisition hype with clear product value, ongoing proof, and member support.
