2. SERP Gap
Most landing pages rely on logos, badges, and vague “audited” language. They rarely show users how to verify the relevant contract, audit scope, locker, allocation, or vesting schedule.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Replace static trust badges with a verifiable trust stack. Each signal should state what it proves, what it does not prove, where it can be checked, and when it was last reviewed.
