2. SERP Gap
Most “top crypto media” lists are static logos, stale contacts, or paid-placement directories. They do not distinguish active reporting from aggregators, editorial from sponsored inventory, or an outlet from the individual reporter’s beat.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Replace the media list with a living newsroom map. It should be reporter-first, date-stamped, evidence-led, and continuously verified rather than claiming a permanent list of outlets with “real reporters.”
