2. SERP Gap
Generic crypto marketing guides use the same press-release formula for every project. They overlook the different evidence burdens, audiences, risk signals, and crisis triggers for exchanges, DeFi protocols, and culture-led memecoins.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
The PR strategy should follow the project’s proof model. Exchanges need eligibility and operational clarity; DeFi needs technical and economic evidence; memecoin work needs clear commercial disclosure and cultural authenticity without inventing utility.
