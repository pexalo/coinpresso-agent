2. SERP Gap
Existing pages frame this as a volume contest or call all distribution worthless. They seldom distinguish announcement record, broad syndication, sponsored content, and independently reported coverage as separate tools.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
The issue is not 50 placements versus one feature; it is whether the communications objective is record-keeping, reach, conversion, or independent validation. Coinpresso can offer a decision matrix rather than a simplistic “wires are bad” argument.
