FAQ Section
Q1: Why do I need a specialized crypto SEO agency? Crypto is classified by Google as a YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) niche, meaning it is subject to the strictest quality and trust algorithms. A specialized crypto SEO agency understands how to build "Cryptographic E-E-A-T," navigate fragmented Web3 site architectures (like dApps and GitBooks), and target high-intent users rather than just chasing low-value vanity traffic.

Q2: How long does it take to see results from crypto SEO? SEO is a long-term strategy. In the highly competitive crypto space, it typically takes 3 to 6 months to see significant movement in rankings and organic traffic, and 6 to 12 months to achieve a strong ROI. Agencies promising overnight #1 rankings are likely using risky "black hat" tactics that will eventually get your site penalized.

Q3: What is the most important ranking factor for crypto websites? While technical SEO and high-quality content are foundational, the most critical differentiator in crypto SEO is Authority (the 'A' in E-E-A-T). This is built through high-quality backlinks from reputable crypto and financial publications, brand mentions, and demonstrable expertise from your team.

Q4: How much does crypto SEO cost in 2026? Quality crypto SEO requires significant resources for technical audits, expert content creation, and premium link building. Most reputable agencies charge monthly retainers ranging from $3,000 to $10,000+, depending on the competitiveness of your niche (e.g., ranking for "crypto exchange" costs vastly more than a niche DeFi tool).

Q5: Does SEO still matter with the rise of AI search engines? Yes, but it's evolving. Traditional SEO builds the foundation (site structure, content quality, authority) that AI engines rely on to generate their answers. The best crypto SEO agencies in 2026 integrate traditional search optimization with Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) to ensure visibility across both Google and LLMs.
