2. SERP Gap
Most guides treat a wallet connect like a completed lead or advise storing identifiers without explaining consent, asynchronous event states, unreliable deterministic matching, or the difference between a click, connection, signature, and confirmed chain event.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Model the conversion journey as states, then measure the minimum necessary. The article should show a privacy-first event taxonomy that separates ad interaction, landing-page action, wallet connection, user rejection, signed intent, and confirmed on-chain event.
