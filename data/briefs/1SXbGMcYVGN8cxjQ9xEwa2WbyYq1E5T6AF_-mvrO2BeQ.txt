Crypto PR Timing for Funding, Listings, and Partnerships

Meta description
Plan funding, listing, and partnership announcements around readiness, embargoes, evidence, audience availability, and follow-through—not a magic day or hour.

2. SERP Gap
Most timing guides recommend a weekday and a morning slot. They neglect the more important gates: the agreement is signed, material facts are approved, partners are aligned, embargo terms are clear, and there is a useful follow-through asset.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Timing is a readiness-sequencing problem, not a calendar hack. Coinpresso can give readers a “proof before publicity” timeline for funding, listings, and partnerships.
