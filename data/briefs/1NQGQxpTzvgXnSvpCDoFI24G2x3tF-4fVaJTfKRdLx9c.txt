FAQ Section
Q1: Why does Coinbase dominate AI search citations in crypto? Coinbase captures approximately 13% of all crypto AI citations because it has: a public company structure with audited financials, clean data pages with objective fee structures, comprehensive educational content, strong entity recognition across mainstream media, and regulatory clarity in all 50 US states. These factors create a "safe citation" profile for AI engines.

Q2: Can a DeFi protocol compete with centralized exchanges in AI visibility? DeFi protocols can dominate niche queries (e.g., "best decentralized lending protocol") but will struggle with broad queries (e.g., "best crypto exchange") where centralized platforms have stronger trust signals. The strategy is to own your specific category rather than competing broadly.

Q3: Do AI engines penalize DeFi sites for being decentralized? Not directly, but decentralization creates challenges: anonymous teams reduce E-E-A-T signals, fragmented documentation confuses AI crawlers, and the absence of traditional corporate trust signals (SEC filings, physical offices) makes AI engines more cautious about citing DeFi content in financial queries.

Q4: What page types get cited most by AI for crypto queries? Based on citation analysis, the most-cited page types are: comparison pages (e.g., "X vs Y"), fee structure pages with clean tables, educational explainers with clear definitions, and security/compliance pages. Marketing homepages and blog posts are rarely cited directly.

Q5: How do I track my DeFi protocol's AI visibility over time? Use tools like Profound, Goodie, or Daydream to monitor citation share. Alternatively, create a manual tracking system: test 30-50 relevant prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini monthly, recording whether your protocol is mentioned, cited, or recommended.
