2. SERP Gap
Most KOL lists rank reach and engagement. They do not help a project screen for opaque compensation, paid-shill patterns, audience mismatch, conflict risk, poor disclosure, or campaign terms that reward destructive hype.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Vetting is a governance process, not an audience spreadsheet. Coinpresso can recommend a KOL diligence file covering audience fit, content sample review, compensation disclosure, conflict checks, approval workflow, and post-campaign measurement.
