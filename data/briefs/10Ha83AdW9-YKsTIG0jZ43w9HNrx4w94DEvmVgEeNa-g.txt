2. SERP Gap
Most moderation guides stop at “use bots” or “hire more people.” They miss the financial stress of crypto communities, false-positive workload, and the way fatigue weakens incident handling and anti-scam discipline.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Treat moderator burnout as an operational-security risk, not only an HR issue. The article should connect workload design, escalation rights, rotation, and post-incident recovery to community safety.

Early warning signals

4
Design a sustainable moderation system
Recommend rotations, escalation tiers, protected breaks, documented decisions, and workload caps.

What bots should reduce—not create
Use automation for clear spam and routing; keep ambiguous cases human-reviewed.

6
Recovery after high-intensity events
Run debriefs, improve SOPs, offer time off, and reset coverage.

Conclusion and FAQ
Provide a moderator-resilience checklist.
