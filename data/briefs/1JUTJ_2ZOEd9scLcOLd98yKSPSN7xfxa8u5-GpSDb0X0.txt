2. SERP Gap
Most comparison pages apply generic performance-marketing advice to crypto. They do not account for restricted products, limited search eligibility, overlapping attribution windows, creative compliance, and the danger of launching channels without a shared measurement design.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
The core choice is a measurement-and-intent decision, not a channel rivalry. Run one channel first when it provides the clearest learning; use both only after objectives, exclusions, and attribution rules can separate their contribution.
