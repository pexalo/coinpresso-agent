FAQ Section
Q1: What does a crypto presale marketing agency do? A crypto presale marketing agency helps blockchain projects raise funds before their public token launch (ICO, IDO, or IEO). Services typically include community building, PR distribution, influencer outreach, technical SEO, paid advertising, and strategic advisory for tokenomics and launchpad selection.

Q2: How much does marketing a crypto presale cost? Presale marketing budgets vary widely based on the project's goals. A foundational campaign might start at $50,000 to $70,000 per month, while aggressive, global token launches often require $100,000 to $150,000+ to cover top-tier PR, extensive KOL networks, and paid media acquisition.

Q3: Why is SEO & GEO important for a crypto presale? SEO & GEO are critical for presales because high-intent investors who are actively searching for new token opportunities are actively utilizing search and answer engines to find the best & safest crypto presales. Unlike social media hype, ranking for terms related to your project's utility builds long-term authority and drives sustainable traffic long after the initial launch phase ends.

Q4: Can I run Google Ads for a crypto presale? Running Google Ads for a crypto presale (like an ICO or IDO) is extremely difficult, as Google classifies these as restricted financial products. However, top crypto marketing agencies know how to navigate these policies, often focusing ads on the project's underlying technology or educational content rather than the token sale itself.

Q5: How long before the launch should I hire a marketing agency? You should hire a crypto presale marketing agency at least 90 to 120 days before your planned Token Generation Event (TGE). This provides enough time to build a genuine community, secure PR placements, optimize search visibility, and generate sustained momentum leading into the sale.
