FAQ Section
Q1: What schema types should a crypto exchange use? A crypto exchange should implement: Organization schema (brand identity), WebSite schema (search functionality), FinancialService schema (service classification), and ExchangeRateSpecification for trading pairs. Additionally, use FAQPage schema on support pages and Article schema on educational content.

Q2: Does schema markup directly improve AI search citations? Schema markup doesn't directly cause AI citations, but it removes ambiguity about what your site is and what it offers. AI crawlers use structured data to classify and understand your content more accurately, which increases the likelihood of being selected as a citation source for relevant queries.

Q3: How is DeFi protocol schema different from exchange schema? DeFi protocols face a unique challenge: they offer financial products without being traditional financial services. The recommended approach is to use FinancialProduct schema for yield-bearing products, SoftwareApplication for the protocol interface, and custom properties for TVL, audit status, and governance structure.

Q4: Should I use FAQ schema on every page of my crypto site? No. FAQ schema should only be applied to pages with genuine, substantive Q&A content. Overusing FAQ schema (especially with thin or promotional answers) can trigger quality filters. Use it strategically on educational pages, product explainers, and support documentation where the questions are genuinely useful.

Q5: How do I validate my crypto site's schema markup? Use Google's Rich Results Test for basic validation, the Crawlux Token Schema Tester for crypto-specific schema, and Schema.org's validator for syntax checking. Test across multiple pages and ensure there are no conflicting entity definitions (e.g., your brand being classified as both an "Organization" and a "SoftwareApplication" at the domain level).
