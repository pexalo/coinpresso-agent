Crypto Presale Marketing Timeline: 90, 30, and 7 Days Before TGE

Meta description
Plan 90, 30, and 7 days before TGE around proof, approvals, operations, community safety, measurement, and post-launch continuity—not last-minute hype.

2. SERP Gap
Most timelines list promotional tasks and countdowns. They fail to sequence the hard gates: evidence readiness, legal approval, technical checks, support coverage, scam protection, partner coordination, and the first post-TGE update.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Treat the timeline as a risk-and-readiness plan, not a hype calendar. Each phase has a non-negotiable evidence, operations, and communications gate before awareness activity scales.
