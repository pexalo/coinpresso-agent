FAQ Section
Q1: What does a Web3 GEO agency actually do? A Web3 Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) agency optimizes your blockchain project to be cited as a source by AI engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude. This involves technical implementations (like llms.txt), structuring data for LLM extraction, building entity authority, and ensuring your content answers complex user queries objectively.

Q2: How is a GEO agency different from a crypto SEO agency? While a crypto SEO agency focuses on ranking your website links on Google's traditional search results, a GEO agency focuses on getting your brand mentioned and recommended inside the AI-generated answers. GEO requires different tactics, prioritizing objective data formatting, third-party validation, and machine-readable trust signals over traditional keyword density.

Q3: Can an agency guarantee citations in ChatGPT or Perplexity? No reputable agency can guarantee citations in AI models, as LLM outputs are probabilistic and depend on the model's training data and RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) systems. However, a top Web3 GEO agency can significantly increase your "reference rate" by formatting your content exactly how AI crawlers prefer to consume it.

Q4: What is the most important technical fix a GEO agency provides? For Web3 projects, the most critical technical implementations are deploying a well-structured llms.txt file to guide AI crawlers through fragmented documentation, and implementing precise JSON-LD schema markup (like FinancialProduct or SoftwareApplication) to clearly define the project's entity to AI engines.

Q5: How long does it take a GEO agency to show results? Because AI models update their training data and search indexes differently than traditional search engines, GEO results can sometimes appear faster than SEO results (within 4-8 weeks for RAG-based engines like Perplexity) but may take months to influence the base weights of foundational models like GPT-4.
