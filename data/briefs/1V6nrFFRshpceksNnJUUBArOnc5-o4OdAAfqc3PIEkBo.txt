2. SERP Gap
Most comparisons assume volume equals signups and treat KOL credibility as automatic. They lack a fair experiment design, transparent commercial-disclosure requirements, and a way to evaluate whether signups are eligible, engaged, and supportable.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Compare the jobs before comparing signups. Clipping can extend durable source material; KOLs can introduce a project through a named voice. A controlled, disclosure-first test should measure audience fit and downstream quality rather than declare a universal winner.
