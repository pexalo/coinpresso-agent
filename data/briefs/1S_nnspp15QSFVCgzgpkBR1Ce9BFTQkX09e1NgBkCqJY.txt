FAQ Section
Q1: What is crypto entity optimization for AI search? Crypto entity optimization is the technical process of structuring your brand's data so that AI search engines (like ChatGPT and Perplexity) recognize your project as a distinct, verifiable entity rather than just a collection of keywords. This involves consistent data formatting, schema markup, and establishing a presence in structured knowledge graphs.

Q2: Why is my crypto project not showing up in ChatGPT? If your project isn't appearing in ChatGPT, it likely suffers from an "identity crisis" or hallucination penalty. This happens when your brand data is inconsistent across your website, GitBook, and social profiles, or when your claims lack verifiable third-party evidence. AI models prefer silence over citing unverified entities.

Q3: How does schema markup help with AI search visibility? Schema markup (specifically Organization and Product schema) provides a standardized, machine-readable format that directly feeds information into AI knowledge graphs. Using the sameAs property in your schema links your website to authoritative profiles (like Crunchbase or GitHub), confirming your entity's identity.

Q4: What is the EAV-E formula in Web3 content? The EAV-E formula stands for Entity-Attribute-Value-Evidence. It is a content structuring method for AI search. Instead of vague claims, you state the Entity (your protocol), the Attribute (transaction speed), the Value (10,000 TPS), and the Evidence (a link to your latest mainnet stress test).

Q5: Do backlinks still matter for AI search engines? Yes, but their role has shifted. For AI search, backlinks from authoritative sites (like major crypto news outlets) act as verification signals that validate your entity's attributes and existence, rather than just passing traditional "link juice" for keyword rankings.
