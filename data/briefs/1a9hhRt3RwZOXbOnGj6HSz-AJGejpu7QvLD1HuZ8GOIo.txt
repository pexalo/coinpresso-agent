Meta description
Track community health with activation, contribution, resolution quality, trust, retention, and signal-to-noise metrics—not just follower and member totals.

2. SERP Gap
Most dashboards report members, followers, messages, and impressions. They do not separate audience size from product activation, useful contribution, moderation quality, support load, safety, or member trust.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Build a community-health scorecard around signal quality and member outcomes. The article maps off-chain conversation to product and contributor behavior without treating wallets or holdings as proof of loyalty.
4. Recommended Article Structure
Section
Suggested H2
What the article must cover

1
Why member count misleads
Explain bots, inactive members, airdrop spikes, and channel overlap.

2
The five KPI families
Activation, contribution, support quality, trust/safety, and retention.

3
Define a signal-to-noise metric
Track useful questions and contributions against spam, duplicate support, and moderation load.

4
Measure moderator operations
Use response SLA, escalation quality, false-positive rate, resolved reports, and staff workload.

5
Connect community to product carefully
Compare feedback themes, event participation, repeat use, and referrals without claiming causal token outcomes.

6
Build a monthly operating review
Set baselines, segment audiences, annotate incidents, and decide actions.

7
Conclusion and FAQ
Include a KPI-scorecard template.
