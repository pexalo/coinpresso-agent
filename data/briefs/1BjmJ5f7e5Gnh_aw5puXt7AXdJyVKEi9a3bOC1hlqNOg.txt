2. SERP Gap
Most bot articles equate automation with moderation. They do not define the boundary between deterministic rule enforcement, AI-assisted triage, high-context community judgment, and safety-critical user support.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Automate noise and routing; keep trust, safety, and judgment human-owned. The article provides an automation boundary matrix rather than a bot feature list.
