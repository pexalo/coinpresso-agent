2. SERP Gap
Most articles present public doxxing as the only alternative to anonymous hype. They do not explain how a team can give journalists meaningful project verification while keeping personal information private.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Shift the story from personal identity to verifiable accountability. A pseudonymous team can build a layered evidence package—code provenance, governance, signer accountability, audit scope, and journalist source access—without promising that anonymity has no trade-offs.
