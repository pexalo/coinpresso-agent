2. SERP Gap
Most agency lists focus on client logos and ROAS claims. They do not help buyers evaluate account ownership, privileged access, billing transparency, tag/container control, policy history, change approvals, or offboarding rights.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Treat paid media as a custody problem as well as a marketing service. The article supplies an account-governance checklist that protects access, evidence, data portability, and policy standing before spend begins.
4. Recommended Article Structure
Section
Suggested H2
What the article must cover

1
Decide who owns what
Set ownership for ad accounts, billing, domains, pixels, tags, analytics, and creative libraries.

2
Use least-privilege access
Grant role-based access; avoid shared credentials and unnecessary admin rights.

3
Ask the policy questions
Request the agency’s approach to certification, destination reviews, documentation, rejections, and official appeals.

4
Inspect tracking and data portability
Confirm GTM, analytics, pixels, audiences, exports, and deletion/transfer rules.

5
Review reporting quality
Expect spend, delivery, disapprovals, attribution limitations, actions, and next decisions—not only ROAS.

6
Plan termination before launch
Document access removal, asset transfer, data retention, and campaign wind-down.

7
Conclusion and FAQ
Provide an agency governance scorecard.
