2. SERP Gap
Most retargeting content begins with pixel installation and urgency sequences. It ignores whether the product can be advertised, whether remarketing is permitted for the financial category, and whether the collection/use of audience data is lawful.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Eligibility precedes sequence design. The article should explain how to build an education-first, consented nurture path only where the offer, market, and platform rules allow it—and when to rely on owned channels instead.
