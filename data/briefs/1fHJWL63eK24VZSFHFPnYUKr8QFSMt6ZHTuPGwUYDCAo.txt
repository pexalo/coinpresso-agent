2. SERP Gap
Most checklists act as if certification is one global form. They overlook country-specific eligibility, product classification, business verification, landing-page accuracy, and the need to re-check requirements after material changes.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Make this a jurisdiction-and-product matrix, not a static certification list. The article should show a repeatable way to establish what is promoted, where, under which entity, with which licensing evidence and disclosure assets.
