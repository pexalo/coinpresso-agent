2. SERP Landscape and Gap
The emerging SERP defines AI share of voice but is still dominated by general B2B marketing explanations and software-led measurement pages. Academic GEO research confirms that visibility in generated answers is multidimensional: citation position, attributed word count, relevance, and influence can all matter, making a single rank-style metric inadequate.1

The opportunity is to publish the first crypto-specific measurement standard. Current results rarely distinguish between a passing brand mention, a source citation, an explicit recommendation, and a negatively framed warning. They also understate response volatility: different runs, prompt phrasings, model versions, search modes, and locations can produce different answers. Finally, they seldom connect AI visibility to outcomes that matter to Web3 teams, such as qualified visits, sign-ups, wallet connections, developer activity, or retained users.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Treat Share of Model Voice as a portfolio of model-level metrics, not one vanity percentage.

Coinpresso should introduce a measurement model that separates mention share, citation share, recommendation share, sentiment, factual accuracy, and prominence. Each metric should be reported by platform and prompt class, with confidence intervals or repeat-run variance. The distinctive value is methodological discipline: the article explains how to build a representative crypto prompt panel and how to avoid claiming progress from a handful of favorable screenshots.
4. Why This Article Can Win
The article offers information gain by turning an immature marketing term into a reproducible measurement protocol. This matters because ChatGPT Search, Gemini grounding, and Claude web search can all retrieve and cite web sources, but their triggering, retrieval, and presentation workflows are not identical.2 3 4

The article should not claim that Share of Model Voice proves causation, predicts token price, or guarantees revenue. It should also avoid publishing universal benchmarks such as “25% is good” without a comparable category, fixed prompt set, model mix, geography, and measurement window.

6. Original Asset or Methodology
Coinpresso can publish a downloadable Crypto Share of Model Voice Scorecard with four components: a prompt inventory, a run log, a model-by-model scoring sheet, and a downstream-outcome worksheet. The prompt inventory should tag every query by category, funnel stage, geography, and risk sensitivity. The run log should record date, model, search mode, and exact prompt. The scorecard should distinguish being named from being cited or recommended, while the outcome sheet should map AI referrals and assisted actions without pretending to prove attribution.
7. FAQ Section
What is Share of Model Voice? Explain it as the proportion of relevant AI answers in which a brand appears relative to a defined competitor set, while noting that the exact formula must be disclosed.

Is an AI mention the same as a citation? No. A mention names the brand; a citation links or attributes information to a source; a recommendation explicitly presents the brand as an option.

How many prompts should a crypto brand track? Recommend a representative panel large enough to cover key intents rather than a universal number; disclose the sample and update it as products and user questions change.

Why should ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude be reported separately? Their search triggers, retrieval systems, interfaces, and citation behavior can differ, so an aggregate can hide platform-specific weaknesses.2 3 4

Can Share of Model Voice be tied to wallet activity? It can be compared with tagged referral and conversion data, but correlation should not be described as proof that AI visibility caused the on-chain action.
