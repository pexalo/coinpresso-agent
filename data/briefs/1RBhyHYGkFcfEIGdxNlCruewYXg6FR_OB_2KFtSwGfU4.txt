2. SERP Gap
Most airdrop articles label users as either “farmers” or “holders.” They omit the operationally useful middle ground: contributors, testers, advocates, developers, and repeat users with different motivations and evidence of value.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Measure contribution quality, not token-holding mythology. The article introduces a segmentation model that distinguishes temporary incentive participation from repeated product use and verifiable contribution without judging users or offering investment advice.

Why “community” is an overloaded metric
