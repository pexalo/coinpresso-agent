2. SERP Landscape and Gap
Current coverage reports that Coinbase and Kraken lead crypto AI citations, then jumps quickly to generic GEO advice. The 5W Crypto & Digital Assets AI Visibility Index tested more than 65 consumer-intent prompts across five AI surfaces and estimated Coinbase at 13% citation share and Kraken at 9% in Q1 2026.1 Those figures are useful but should be presented as results from one disclosed prompt panel—not as a universal market share measurement.

The missing analysis is how the two moats differ. Coinbase is associated with scale, U.S. availability, regulatory and public-company evidence; Kraken is associated with longevity, security, and proof-of-reserves narratives in the 5W analysis.1 Smaller projects cannot reproduce those histories, but they can copy the publishing mechanics and target narrower recommendation contexts.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Do not copy the giants’ breadth; copy their evidence architecture and find a prompt wedge they do not own.

The article should decompose each brand’s citation moat into five layers—category association, verifiable evidence, third-party repetition, query coverage, and freshness—then show smaller projects how to win a narrow set of high-fit prompts. This is more actionable than another general article about how exchanges are evaluated.
4. Why This Article Can Win
The article combines a recognizable case study with a realistic challenger strategy. It should reproduce the source methodology clearly, including prompt count, platforms, time period, and the index’s own disclaimer that citation share is not a safety or investment ranking.1

Coinpresso should avoid suggesting that machine-readable compliance documents alone create citations. The stronger argument is cumulative: clear first-party evidence gives journalists, reviewers, directories, and search-grounded systems something consistent to reference. The article should also distinguish what smaller projects can copy from structural advantages they cannot copy, such as age, market scale, or public-company reporting.

7. FAQ Section
Do Coinbase and Kraken really account for 22% of crypto AI citations? State that this is the estimate from 5W’s Q1 2026 test set of 65+ consumer prompts across several AI platforms, not a universal census.1

Why might Coinbase and Kraken appear frequently? Discuss brand history, independently verifiable information, category association, regulatory/security narratives, and repeated third-party coverage without claiming access to proprietary model weights.

Can a smaller token or exchange outrank Coinbase in AI answers? It may win narrower, better-matched prompts, but should not expect to reproduce broad incumbent recognition quickly.

What is a prompt wedge? A focused recommendation context in which the smaller project has a documented, meaningful advantage and sufficient evidence.

What should a project measure after publishing evidence modules? Track prompt-specific mentions, citations, recommendation context, accuracy, and model variance over repeated tests.
