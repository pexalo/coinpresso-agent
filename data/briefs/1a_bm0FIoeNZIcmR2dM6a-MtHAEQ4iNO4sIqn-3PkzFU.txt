2. SERP Gap
Most pitching advice focuses on subject lines and follow-up cadence. It misses the crypto-specific need for verifiable source material: contract information, clear funding terms, data methodology, regulatory context, limitations, and access to a decision-maker.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
A pitch is read when it reduces reporting work. Build a “source utility” package that makes the event timely, verifiable, contextual, and easy to investigate—whether contact comes from a founder or a PR representative.

What journalists need from a crypto pitch

Follow up without creating noise
Use a brief, useful update and respect a reporter’s stated preferences.
