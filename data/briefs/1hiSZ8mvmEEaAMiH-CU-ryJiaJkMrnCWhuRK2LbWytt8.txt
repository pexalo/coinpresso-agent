2. SERP Gap
Most explainers define the models but treat marketing as the same hype engine. They do not show how each model changes the promise, access process, proof burden, and post-launch relationship with the community.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Match the marketing promise to the allocation mechanism. The article uses a launch-model fit matrix so no campaign claims fairness, access, scarcity, or decentralization beyond what the design actually supports.
