2. SERP Gap
Most crypto PR pages oversell links or incorrectly state that all press-release links are dofollow/no-follow. They do not teach buyers to inspect the final placement: label, byline, canonical URL, links, indexation, and editorial status.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Make this a placement-forensics guide, not a journalist-mind-reading article. The value is a repeatable audit of what the publisher actually published and what the link labels mean.
