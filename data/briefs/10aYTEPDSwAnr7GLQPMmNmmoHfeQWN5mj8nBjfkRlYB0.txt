2. SERP Gap
Most cost articles invent “average” figures or focus only on KOL and ad spend. They omit legal/compliance review, technical evidence, analytics, scam prevention, community operations, and post-TGE retention.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Use a capability-based budget, not a magic number. The article gives a scenario planner that allocates spend by mission-critical capabilities and makes the cost of skipping each category visible.
