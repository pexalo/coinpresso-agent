2. SERP Gap
Existing pages optimize for sign-up volume and social tasks. They rarely show how teams should document eligibility, capacity, selection, allocation, referral treatment, risk, or meaningful changes.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Make scarcity verifiable and eligibility legible. The article reframes FOMO as clear access rules, real capacity constraints, and transparent allocation logic rather than countdown tricks or pressure tactics.
