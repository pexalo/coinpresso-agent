2. SERP Gap
Most category guides recycle one presale funnel. They do not compare the different proof burdens: technical adoption and ecosystem readiness for Layer-1s, cultural participation and clear risk boundaries for memecoins, and legal/asset/operational transparency for RWA tokens.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Buyer psychology follows the proof model. The article provides a three-column message architecture that starts with the questions each audience must be able to verify, not with a stereotype about investor emotion.
