FAQ Section
Q1: What is Google Ads Quality Score? Quality Score is a diagnostic tool (rated 1-10) that Google uses to measure the relevance and quality of your ads, keywords, and landing pages. A higher Quality Score leads to lower costs per click (CPCs) and better ad positions.

Q2: Why are my crypto Google Ads so expensive? Crypto Google Ads are often expensive because the industry is highly competitive and many crypto advertisers suffer from low Quality Scores. If your landing page experience is poor or your ad relevance is low, Google forces you to pay a higher CPC to maintain your ad position.

Q3: How does landing page experience affect crypto ads? Landing page experience is a major component of Quality Score. If you send paid traffic to a confusing Web3 interface, a site that loads slowly, or a page lacking clear compliance disclaimers, Google will rate the experience as "Below Average," significantly increasing your ad costs.

Q4: Can I improve my Quality Score without changing my bids? Yes. Improving your ad copy to increase Expected CTR, tightly grouping your keywords for better Ad Relevance, and optimizing your landing page for user experience will improve your Quality Score, lowering your CPCs without requiring you to increase your bids.

Q5: How do Google's crypto advertising policies impact Quality Score? Google's strict policies on restricted financial products mean crypto advertisers cannot use aggressive, clickbait language (e.g., "guaranteed returns"). This can lower Expected CTR if not handled creatively. Advertisers must balance compliance with compelling copywriting to maintain a high Quality Score.
