2. SERP Gap
Existing lists test social-posting skills and generic engagement ideas. They miss the operational reality of crypto communities: scam reports, compromise escalation, uncertain information, on-chain literacy, and protected member communication.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Hire for judgment under uncertainty, not audience hype. The article gives structured scenario questions and a scorecard for evidence handling, security escalation, writing, empathy, and operational discipline.
4. Recommended Article Structure
Section
Suggested H2
What the article must cover

1
Define the role before the interview
Separate moderator, support lead, community strategist, ambassador manager, and social manager.

2
Use scenario-based questions
Test a fake-admin report, market panic, roadmap delay, recurring scam link, and heated governance discussion.

3
Score evidence discipline
Assess when the candidate verifies, escalates, documents, and avoids speculation.

4
Assess crypto literacy safely
Test ability to interpret public documentation and identify when to hand off—not to give wallet advice.

5
Test communication craft
Review a short incident update, FAQ answer, and de-escalation message.

6
Run a paid practical exercise
Use a synthetic scenario with clear consent and no access to sensitive tools.

7
Conclusion and FAQ
Include an interview-scorecard template.
