FAQ Section
Q1: What is the difference between crypto PR and crypto marketing? Crypto marketing focuses on direct user acquisition, lead generation, and community growth (often through paid ads, SEO, or KOLs). Crypto PR (Public Relations) focuses on managing your brand's reputation, securing media coverage in journalistic outlets, handling crisis communications, and building long-term credibility with investors and the public.

Q2: How much does a top crypto PR agency charge? Top-tier crypto PR agencies typically operate on monthly retainers ranging from $5,000 to $15,000+. Some agencies offer "pay-for-placement" models where you only pay when an article is published, but retainers are standard for comprehensive media relations, strategy, and crisis management.

Q3: Can a PR agency guarantee coverage in CoinDesk or Cointelegraph? No ethical PR agency can guarantee earned (editorial) coverage in top-tier journalistic outlets like CoinDesk or Decrypt, as those decisions are made by independent editors. However, agencies can guarantee sponsored content or press release syndication on those platforms. The best agencies have strong relationships with journalists to pitch your story effectively.

Q4: Why is PR important for crypto SEO and AI visibility? PR is the foundation of off-page SEO and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO). When high-authority sites mention your brand or link to your website, it signals to Google and AI engines (like ChatGPT) that your project is legitimate and authoritative. This "Cryptographic E-E-A-T" is essential for ranking in competitive Web3 searches.

Q5: What makes a good crypto PR pitch? Journalists ignore generic product launches. A good crypto PR pitch focuses on industry impact, novel technological breakthroughs, significant institutional partnerships, or expert commentary on current market trends. Your PR agency should help you find the "hook" that makes your project newsworthy.
