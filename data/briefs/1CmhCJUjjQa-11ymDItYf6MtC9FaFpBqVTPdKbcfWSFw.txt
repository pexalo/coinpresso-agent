2. SERP Gap
Most checklists list documents without showing how to make them reviewable, current, internally owned, or specific to each platform and target market. They also ignore the privacy and tracking documentation that campaigns may need.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Build a living source-of-truth, not a PDF dump. The dossier should be indexed by platform, legal entity, product, country, domain, claim, and update date so it supports both a pre-launch review and factual appeal.

Provide a dossier index template.

Avoid promising approval, reinstatement, delivery, signups, ROI, or token-related outcomes.
