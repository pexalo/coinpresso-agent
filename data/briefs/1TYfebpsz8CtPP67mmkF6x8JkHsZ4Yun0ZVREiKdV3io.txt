Meta description
Build crawlable, functional crypto PPC landing pages with clear offer information, accessible disclosures, policy-consistent claims, and no deceptive destination behavior.

2. SERP Gap
Most pages focus on ad text and mention the landing page only as a design asset. They miss crawlability, destination functionality, disclosure accessibility, consistent claims, and the need to identify what links and scripts a reviewer can encounter.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Treat the landing page as a reviewable evidence surface, not a conversion-only page. The article should pair conversion design with a destination audit that tests accessibility, claim consistency, key linked materials, and user safety.
