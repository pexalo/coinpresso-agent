2. SERP Gap
Current compliance content lists banned words or speaks only in abstractions. It rarely shows a communications workflow that maps each material statement to substantiation, jurisdiction, audience, required qualifier, owner, and approval.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Treat every material PR claim as a version-controlled record. The article proposes a claim register so communications teams can distinguish factual product statements from projections, testimonials, paid promotion, legal conclusions, and risk-sensitive language.
