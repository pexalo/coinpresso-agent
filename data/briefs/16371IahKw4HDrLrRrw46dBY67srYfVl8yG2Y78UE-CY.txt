2. SERP Gap
The usual debate asks which side gets more pickup, with little evidence. It ignores the operating model: founders own insight and accountability; agencies add process, media intelligence, editorial discipline, and capacity.
3. Unique Coinpresso Angle
Compare operating systems, not mythical pickup rates. The right choice depends on source access, evidence maturity, message complexity, approval speed, and the repeatability required—not on whether the sender’s signature says founder or agency.
