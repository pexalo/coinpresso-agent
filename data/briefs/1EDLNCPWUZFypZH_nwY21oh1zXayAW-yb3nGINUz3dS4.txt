FAQ Section
Q1: Why is crypto PPC so difficult compared to traditional paid advertising? Crypto PPC is challenging because major platforms like Google, Meta, and TikTok have strict, frequently changing policies regarding financial products, cryptocurrencies, and token sales. A specialized crypto PPC agency knows how to structure landing pages, ad copy, and account setups to ensure compliance and prevent account bans.

Q2: How much should a Web3 project budget for a crypto PPC agency? In 2026, most top-tier crypto PPC agencies require a minimum monthly ad spend of $5,000 to $10,000, plus an agency management fee (typically 10-20% of spend or a flat retainer). Projects should be prepared for higher CPCs (Cost Per Click) than traditional industries due to the competitive nature of Web3 keywords.

Q3: Can crypto projects run ads on Google and Facebook? Yes, but with restrictions. Google allows ads for cryptocurrency exchanges and wallets if the advertiser is certified and meets local regulatory requirements. Meta (Facebook/Instagram) requires prior written permission for many crypto products. A specialized agency like Coinpresso navigates these certification processes for you.

Q4: What is a good ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) for crypto campaigns? ROAS varies wildly by niche. A crypto exchange acquiring high-LTV traders might target a 300-400% ROAS over 6 months, while a DeFi protocol might focus on Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) for wallet connections. The best agencies focus on full-funnel tracking to measure actual on-chain conversions, not just clicks.

Q5: Should we use Web3-native ad networks or traditional platforms? A balanced strategy uses both. Traditional platforms (Google, X) offer massive scale and intent-based targeting, while Web3-native networks (like Slise or Hype) offer highly targeted, crypto-native audiences. Your agency should allocate budget based on your specific user acquisition goals.
